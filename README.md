## 👨‍💻 Engineering Profile

I am a Software Engineer and Mobile Application Developer specializing in cross-platform systems using **Flutter & Dart**, backed by modern cloud and native runtimes (**Firebase, Node.js, SQLite, MongoDB, REST/WebSockets**). My engineering focus centers on clean architecture, low-latency UI execution (60/120fps), and resilient edge systems.

### Core Architectural Competencies
- **Local-First & Offline Synchronization**: Architecting robust offline caches with **SQLite / Sqflite**, **Drift**, and **Hive** with background sync workers that prevent data loss in low-connectivity retail, warehouse, and field operations.
- **AI Integrations & Computer Vision**: Embedding **OpenAI & Google Gemini APIs** for multimodal video content analysis, script/hook synthesis, and automated financial chart reasoning.
- **Hardware Protocols & Field Mobility**: Integrating **ESC/POS thermal printer** protocols across Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and USB channels, camera barcode/QR engines, and GPS geofence validation.
- **Real-Time Telemetry & Geospatial Logistics**: Engineering turn-by-turn routing, high-frequency vehicle telemetry, and dynamic dispatch over Google Maps Platform and WebSockets.
- **Monetization & App Lifecycle**: Implementing in-app subscriptions with **RevenueCat & IAP**, programmatic ads with **Google AdMob**, OTA Dart code patches with **Shorebird**, and continuous delivery to the App Store and Google Play.
