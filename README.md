## 📌 About Me

I am a Software Engineer and Mobile Application Developer specializing in cross-platform engineering with **Flutter & Dart**, backed by modern backends (**Firebase, Node.js, SQLite, MongoDB**). 

My engineering focus spans:
- **Local-First & Offline Architecture**: Designing resilient apps using SQLite/Sqflite, Drift, and Hive with conflict-free background synchronizations.
- **Real-Time Systems & Telemetry**: Building live tracking, Google Maps navigation, dynamic dispatch, and WebSocket-driven chat solutions.
- **Enterprise & App Ecosystems**: Multi-role platforms (customer, driver, operator, enterprise manager) with end-to-end store deployment on Google Play and Apple App Store.
- **Clean Architecture & Scalability**: Clean code, MVVM/Layered patterns, and decoupled state management (BLoC, GetX, Provider, Riverpod).
